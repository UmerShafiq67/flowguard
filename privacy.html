Privacy Policy

StudySprint is designed to keep study data under the student's control.

Data we use

The app can store subjects, topics, exam dates, plans, practice evidence, mistakes, focus history, and account information needed to provide the service. AI requests may include the study context needed to answer the student's request.

Local storage

Study data can be stored locally in the browser so the app continues to work without an account.

Cloud accounts

When an account is configured and the student signs in, StudySprint can sync study data to the configured account database. Access is restricted to the signed-in account by database security rules.

Payments

Subscription checkout and payment processing are handled by our payment provider. StudySprint does not place payment card secrets in browser configuration or application storage.

AI

AI requests are sent to the configured StudySprint AI service. StudySprint only sends the request and study context needed for the requested feature, subject to the service configuration.

Choices

You can use the local-first app without creating an account. Signed-in users can request account actions available in the Account section, including cloud-data deletion.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support/contact channel made available by StudySprint.
