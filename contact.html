StudySprint Support

Need help with your study plan, account, or subscription?

Account help

Open StudySprint and use the Account section for sign-in, sync, password reset, and cloud-data controls.

Billing help

For a payment or subscription issue, use the support/contact channel associated with your StudySprint purchase and include the transaction details requested there. Never send a password, secret API key, or payment-card details.

Product help

For a study-product issue, include the page you were using and a short description of what happened. Your local study data can be exported or restored through the app's available backup controls.
