Refund Policy

StudySprint Plus is a recurring digital subscription.

Subscription billing

Charges are shown before checkout. A subscription renews according to the billing interval selected at checkout unless it is canceled.

Refund requests

If you believe you were charged in error or need help with a purchase, contact StudySprint through the support/contact channel available with your purchase. Payment-provider rules may also apply to the transaction.

Cancellation

You can use the subscription-management options provided by the payment provider to stop future renewals. Cancellation does not by itself erase study data stored locally or in your StudySprint account.
